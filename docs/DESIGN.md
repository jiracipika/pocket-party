# Design direction
Pocket Party is a pocket arcade: bold toy-box art and three distinct mechanics, not a SaaS dashboard.
Base #0a0a0f, cards #1c1c2e, text #faf9ff, mint #7dffb2, yellow #ffe66d, lilac #bd9cff; coral sticker supplies the one expressive flourish.
Heavy condensed-feeling Arial Black headlines, readable Arial body. Oversized left-aligned Big fun / Tiny games headline, comic burst to right, illustrated cartridges in three columns below. On phone the cartridges stack.
Opaque scene illustrations actually depict each game's mechanic. Consistent play buttons, gesture descriptions and bounded hover feedback. Reduced-motion disables decorative loops. Native availability disclosure is explicit.
Review against brief: no generic metrics, feature grids, fake players, fake leaderboard or unnecessary account system. Shared night palette follows user's existing preference; game-specific playful art carries identity.
